01 /ABOUT MXI

Understand the world.
Learn to interact with it.

MXI stands for Mathematics x Intelligence. Our work connects ideas, models, and engineering, with embodied AI as one place to put them into practice: learning from observations and demonstrations, and turning understanding into action.

By connecting data, models, and evaluation through open engineering, we work to bring algorithms closer to the physical world.

Get to know our open-source work↗
02 /RESEARCH INTERESTS

Exploring what intelligence can do.

From observation to action. From models to systems.
Three complementary directions in embodied AI.

01

PERCEPTION → ACTION

Vision, language, action

Connecting visual observations, language instructions, and robot actions to explore how multimodal understanding becomes learned skills.

Explore this direction+

Starting with demonstrations, we build reproducible experiments around policy post-training, action representations, and inference interfaces.

02

WORLD → MODEL

World & action models

Investigating the connection between environment representations and action generation, with models built for physical interaction.

Explore this direction+

Our open-source foundation keeps an independent custom-model backend, connecting different model approaches through shared data and interfaces.

03

LEARNING → EVIDENCE

Training & evaluation

Connecting data preparation, post-training, inference services, and simulation evaluation to make experiments and results traceable.

Explore this direction+

We record configurations, logs, and checkpoints, using original evaluation artifacts to distinguish a working pipeline from demonstrated task capability.
